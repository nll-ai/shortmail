Benefits of using ShortMail

  • Fewer emails: Some people will find their answer in your FAQ or will realize that it's not that important after all.
  • Shorter emails: Unsolicited messages sent via contact form are limited to 500 characters.
  • Better emails: People will understand how you prefer email and will follow your requests.
  • Organized inbox: Use filters in your email client to group and sort messages, for instance by topic.
  • Analytics: See how many people have visited your ShortMail page but have decided not to email you.
  • No software: There's nothing to install. ShortMail works with every email client.

About ShortMail

ShortMail is a tool designed to help people manage high email loads effectively. It provides a simple contact form and a place to tell the world how you prefer to receive email. By setting clear expectations and providing essential information upfront, ShortMail helps you receive fewer and better emails, saving you time and reducing email-related stress.

Inspired by the now-defunct Shortwhale, ShortMail brings you a streamlined, user-friendly solution to take control of your inbox.
