# Surface brief — Landing (/)

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page IS an airmail envelope on a morning desk — the visitor writes
a short letter, presses the postmark, and watches it depart; the category
default (hero headline + subcopy + three feature cards) is refused.

OWN-WORLD: warm laid paper ground (#f7f2e7, faint ruled lines), ink black
(#1a1613), airmail hatch red/blue (#b3402a / #2a4b8d) as the envelope edge,
one vermillion stamp accent; type: self-hosted typewriter face (Courier
Prime) for the letter, stamped caps for the postmark; perforated edges and
the round postmark are authored CSS from the postal world.

STORY: "I write a short letter on real paper, press the postmark, and it
leaves for Eric's desk — 500 characters, no forms, no friction." The visitor
also learns the rule: Eric's subjects tell you what he wants to hear about.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The envelope fills the first viewport as the page itself:
hatched airmail border frames everything; top-right, a perforated stamp
denominated "500 CHARACTERS"; top-left, a circular postmark reading
SHORTMAIL POST. Center: the letter begins "Dear Eric," — ruled lines carry
sender name, return address, a subject chosen from Eric's headings, and the
message body with a live character counter. The letterhead reads Eric's
promise: "500 characters. I read every one." Primary action: the SEND
POSTMARK press at the letter's foot.

FORM: code-led (no image generation available). Seed: f230bb36.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
